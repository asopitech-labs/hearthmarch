Field notes

    Water is impassable. Woods slow a march. Standing on a tile claims it. A hearth shelters its own people, so a fight on the cottage is harder than a fight in the open. Leave your hearth empty and Reedmoor will walk in. Faith comes from the villages, and a hard tax stills it. Drag the marsh, or use WASD and the arrow keys, to look around. The wheel and the + − buttons zoom. Click the small map to jump. Left-click or drag a box to select marchers. Shift-click adds one. Right-click sends them, and a dotted path shows the way. On a touch screen, drag to look, pinch to zoom, tap a marcher to select, and tap the ground to send them. Space pauses. F changes the pace. 1, 2, and 3 ready that god's rites. H looks back at your hearth. E selects every marcher you can see. Escape clears the selection.
