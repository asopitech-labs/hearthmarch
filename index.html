Field notes

    Water is impassable. Woods slow a march. Standing on a tile claims it. A hearth shelters its own people, so a fight on the cottage is harder than a fight in the open. Leave your hearth empty and Reedmoor will walk in. Pause with the button or Space. Pace 2× hurries the clock.
