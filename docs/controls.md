# Looking around and giving orders

The wide marsh is larger than the window, so the view is a camera. The gestures follow two published real-time strategy layouts, then a touch adaptation for a screen that has no right button.

## What the references actually say

**StarCraft II.** Blizzard's simplified-controls guide says the left mouse button selects a unit, and dragging that button selects several. Shift plus a selection adds or removes units. A right click on the ground orders the selection to move. The arrow keys scroll the map, holding the middle mouse button scrolls it as well, and a click on the minimap moves the camera. Escape clears the selection. Page Up and Page Down zoom the view; the mouse wheel does that in single-player, observer, and replay modes.

**Age of Empires IV.** World's Edge and Xbox Wire published the same split: left click selects, Shift and left click adds or removes, a left-button drag is a bandbox, and a right click issues the contextual order. The arrow keys pan, with Alt+WASD as the secondary binding. H centers the camera on the town center. A later patch noted that zooming out too far hurt selection and readability, which is why this camera stops at a near limit and a far limit instead of showing the whole marsh as specks.

## How that sits on this marsh

- Drag the map with the middle button, or with one finger. WASD and the arrow keys pan too, and the pointer pans when it rests on the edge of the map.
- The wheel, a pinch, and the + and − buttons zoom. The limits keep a cottage readable and still show more than one screen.
- The small map in the corner is the whole marsh. Click or drag it to jump the camera. H looks back at your hearth.
- Left click selects a marcher. Shift-click adds or removes one. A left-button drag draws a box. Double-click, the All button, or E selects every marcher currently in view.
- Right click sends the selection. They take neighboring squares instead of stacking on one tile, and a dotted line previews the path before you click.
- On a touch screen, a tap selects and a tap on open ground sends, because there is no right button. A drag looks around and a pinch zooms.
- Space pauses. F changes the pace. 1, 2, and 3 ready that god's three rites. A prepared rite is aimed with a left click. Escape clears the selection and the rite.

Reedmoor keeps calling rites through the match. A cast leaves enough faith for a smaller rite when one is available, and an empty bowl eases the tax so the villages can pray again.
